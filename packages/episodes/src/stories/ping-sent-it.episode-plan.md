# Ping Sent It

**Status:** Pilot production plan  
**Master:** 21 seconds, 1080×1920, 30 fps  
**Series:** PINGS  
**Story surface:** WhatsApp on one iPhone

## Episode promise

Ping delivers everything instantly and only asks where after the damage is done.

- **Audience:** startup teams, managers, creators, and anyone who has sent something to the wrong chat
- **Primary emotion:** recognition-driven cringe followed by false relief
- **Viewer question:** where did Ping send the file?
- **Novelty:** the chat carries literal information while three performers stage confidence, judgment, and authority around the phone
- **Share trigger:** “This is literally the person who ships before reading the ticket.”
- **Aftertaste:** Ping has learned absolutely nothing
- **Loop:** Teal’s final “...where?” returns naturally to Ping’s opening “Delivered ✅”

## Exact chat script

The group is already open at frame zero.

1. **Ping / seeded outgoing:** `Delivered ✅`
2. **Teal / 1.3s:** `Ping. Where did you send it?`
3. **Ping / typed, sent at 4.3s:** `Launch Room?`
4. **Teal / 6.3s:** `That room has 2,431 people.`
5. **Violet / typing 9.4–10.35s**
6. **Violet / 10.5s:** `Good.`
7. **Violet / 12.7s:** `Now send the corrected file.`
8. **Ping / typed, sent at 15.5s:** `Delivered ✅`
9. **Teal / typing 17.6–18.25s**
10. **Teal / 18.4s:** `…where?`

The story must remain fully understandable with audio muted.

## Performer choreography

- Ping occupies the lower center, visually attached to the device rather than floating above it.
- Teal holds lower-left and Violet lower-right, both outside the main reading lane.
- Reactions begin after the causal message becomes legible.
- Ping uses approved v2 pet animations: `review`, `waiting`, `failed`, `jumping`, `running`, then `failed`.
- Teal transitions `calm → suspicious → judgmental`.
- Violet transitions `composed → verdict`, with less movement than either subordinate.
- Only Ping receives elastic motion. The power characters become funnier by doing less.

## Voice and sound

- Use the deterministic `ping-courier`, `teal-manager`, and `violet-founder` voices.
- Ping’s repeated `pim-billo` marks delivery; repetition builds recognition and makes the final mistake funnier.
- Teal’s final one-syllable `hm` lands after the message, followed by silence.
- Violet’s `Good.` is the cleanest and most isolated cue in the scene.
- Native send, receive, typing, and keyboard sounds remain quieter than foreground blurbs.
- Use restrained ambient music; avoid a large comedy sting that tells the audience how to feel.

## Palette and lighting

- **Ground:** dark navy-to-muted-violet Signal Pop field
- **Plot color:** warm white WhatsApp surface with coral outgoing bubbles
- **Ping anchor:** coral orange and deep navy
- **Teal anchor:** controlled teal
- **Violet anchor:** deep plum
- **Tension accent:** warning coral already owned by Ping, intensified by contrast rather than recoloring him
- **Payoff accent:** restrained warm gold around Violet’s reversal
- Keep the quietest background region behind the phone and faces.

## Camera

- Begin wide enough to understand the three-person hierarchy.
- Move to the exact Teal question, then to the keyboard.
- Hold the `2,431 people` message long enough to read and process.
- Use a restrained push during Violet’s typing pause.
- Give `Good.` a clean authority close-up.
- Return to the keyboard for the repeated delivery behavior.
- End on Teal’s exact `…where?` bubble and hold; no exit flourish.

## Thumbnail and publishing hook

- **Thumbnail frame:** Ping celebrating beside the phone while Teal looks judgmental and `2,431 people` is readable.
- **Title:** `Ping Sent It`
- **Opening caption:** `He never asks where.`
- **Comment prompt:** `Who is Ping on your team?`
- **Series marker:** every PINGS episode opens or closes on a notification/delivery behavior Ping confidently misunderstands.

## Acceptance gates

- The first message and character hierarchy are readable inside two seconds.
- No performer covers the active message, typing indicator, composer, keyboard, or status bar.
- Ping’s repeated voice and motion remain recognizable across both deliveries.
- `Good.` reads as a genuine reversal, not missing context.
- Final `…where?` holds for at least 2.2 seconds and creates a clean loop.
- The episode passes package tests, asset provenance validation, workspace validation, and one real MP4 render with visual review.
