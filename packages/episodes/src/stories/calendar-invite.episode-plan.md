# Episode Plan: The Calendar Invite

## Identity

- Episode ID: `calendar-invite`
- Series: Workplace Signals
- Status: production draft
- Target duration: 24 seconds
- Master aspect ratio: 9:16 at 1080×1920
- Target platforms: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok
- App/device: iMessage and iOS Calendar notification on iPhone 16

## Promise

- One-sentence premise: Coral accidentally invites Teal to a private performance review, and Teal
  responds with a review invitation of their own.
- Audience: people who work in teams and have experienced dangerous calendar hygiene
- Primary emotion: social dread turning into a controlled power reversal
- Share trigger: “This is exactly something our team would do.”
- Intended aftertaste: Teal quietly wins without raising their voice.

## Beat Sheet

| Time | Story beat | New information | Emotional change | Power change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Irreversible notification | Teal accepted their own performance-review invite | Coral becomes exposed | Coral loses control |
| 3–7.4s | Confrontation | Violet confirms Coral added Teal | Coral moves from confusion to panic | Violet judges |
| 7.4–10.8s | Teal enters | Teal is looking forward to the feedback | Teal becomes amused | Teal claims the situation |
| 10.8–15s | Countermove | Teal sends a calendar invite about Coral | Coral's attempted explanation fails | Teal reverses the review |
| 15–18.2s | Held breath | Violet starts, stops, then restarts typing | Everyone waits | Violet decides |
| 18.2–24s | Verdict | Violet accepts; Teal says “See you Friday.” | Coral is defeated | Teal and Violet align |

## Voice and Sound

- Calendar notification: bright native orienting cue and one device recoil
- iMessage sends: native message cues
- Teal typing: short controlled loop
- Violet typing: start, silence, restart
- Coral blurbs: elastic rising uncertainty
- Teal blurbs: clipped, restrained amusement
- Violet blurbs: minimal falling phrases surrounded by silence
- Background music: low warm bed, reduced during 15–18.2-second suspense
