# Cinematic Subjects

Cinematic subjects are typed camera targets projected by the package that owns their geometry.

## Why They Exist

Episodes should focus on meaningful UI regions, not brittle pixel coordinates.

Examples:

- device body, display, keyboard, notification, or OS surface
- app semantic regions such as `header` or `input_area`
- exact entities such as a message bubble or its media fragment
- structured groups of subjects

## Role In The System

- app packages expose versioned schemas and project from canonical headless layouts
- device packages project physical and OS-owned surfaces from registered profiles
- camera resolves subjects before React painting and records exact provenance in its trace
- episodes target subjects with explicit missing-subject behavior

Never derive camera targets from DOM measurement or guessed fallback rectangles.
